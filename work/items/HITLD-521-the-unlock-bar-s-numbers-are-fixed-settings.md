## Question

Are the unlock bar's numbers (15 merges, 7 days, 8 distinct items) fixed settings, or does the system learn them?

HITLD-478 set the bar; HITLD-472 left open whether any learned parameter remains and where it lives (HITLD-408). Design 4.6: the system never grants itself autonomy.

## Options and recommendation

- A. Fixed settings, changes proposed: The numbers live in policy as the owner's settings; the measuring in HITLD-408 may propose a change from evidence, and only the owner changes them
- B. Learned by the system: The system tunes them and reports
- C. Fixed, never proposed: Nothing suggests changes

Recommended: Fixed settings, changes proposed. The chief of staff recommended it.
