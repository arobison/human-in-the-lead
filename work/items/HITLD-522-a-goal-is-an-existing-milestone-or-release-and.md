## Question

What is a goal in the tracker, and where does its budget live?

HITLD-503: the unit of commitment autonomy is a goal under a budget. HITLD-291 left open what a goal is in the tracker and who sets the budget.

## Options and recommendation

- A. An existing milestone or release; budget in a signed decision: A goal is a milestone or release item with its outcome and checkable done-when clauses; the budget (tokens, time, attention) lives in a commitment decision the owner signs with the goal and the rung for each kind of commitment; a new budget is a new decision carrying the proof from HITLD-515
- B. A new kind of record: A commitment record holds goal, budget and rungs
- C. Budget on the milestone itself: A milestone gains a budget field

Recommended: An existing milestone or release; budget in a signed decision. The chief of staff recommended it.
