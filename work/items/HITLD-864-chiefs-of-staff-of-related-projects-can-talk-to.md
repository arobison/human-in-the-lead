## Why

The owner finds it useful for the chiefs of staff of related projects to be able to talk to each other, and asked on 2026-11-22 to think about how that works alongside the collaboration-with-humans model and how humans collaborate with each other. Today a chief of staff talks to its own owner, its own orchestrators and its own executors; nothing lets two projects' chiefs of staff exchange messages, and nothing says what the people involved see of it or may approve.

## Questions to settle first

- Who authorizes a cross-project conversation: each project's owner once (a standing link), or per topic?
- What may cross: questions and status only, or also requests for work, shared decisions, or a shared item?
- Do the owners see the exchange (a mirrored record in each tracker), and does a message from another project's chief of staff count as data or as a request, given that approvals come only from an owner's own words?
- How does it fit the model of people collaborating with each other (two owners, a team): is a chief-of-staff link a convenience for the owners' own conversation, or a channel of its own with its own record?
- What stays private to each project (its private store, its private terms) when two projects talk.

## Done when

- A design note (design part to be named) answers the questions above and records the owner's choices as decisions. This is a thinking item: it plans nothing to build yet.
