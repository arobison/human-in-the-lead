## Question

Handover thresholds for orchestrators: what should the defaults be, and who measures context?

Orch-5 reached about 600k tokens and about 61 dollars, more than twice all eight archived executors combined, with nothing enforcing the 0.7 context share, which the orchestrator must report itself.

## Options and recommendation

- A. Measured by the chief of staff, share 0.3, 8 completions: The chief of staff reads the real context use from the session record at each wake and check-in and makes handover-due true past 30 percent or 8 completions; dispatch start refuses until the orchestrator hands over; this repository's policy is set now and the package defaults drop to 0.3 and 8.
- B. Lower defaults only, still self-reported: 0.3 and 8, honor system for the share.
- C. Keep the defaults: 0.7 and 20.

Recommended: Measured by the chief of staff, share 0.3, 8 completions. The chief of staff recommended it.
