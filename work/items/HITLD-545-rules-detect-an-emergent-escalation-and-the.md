## Question

Who decides that something is an emergent escalation that jumps ahead of planned work?

HITLD-441: on 2026-11-16 several problems that had to jump the queue were handled by hand, each differently (a wrong token record holding every start, a phantom dispatch holding both slots, a stale check making the merge rule ambiguous).

## Options and recommendation

- A. Rules detect, the chief of staff declares the rest: A closed list of mechanical conditions the system detects raises an escalation automatically; for anything else the chief of staff declares one with a reason from the same list; the owner is told and can demote it; the planner puts escalations first and keeps one slot reserved for them even when the pace line says slow
- B. The chief of staff declares only: No automatic detection
- C. Only the owner declares: Nothing jumps the queue unless the owner says so

Recommended: Rules detect, the chief of staff declares the rest. The chief of staff recommended it.
