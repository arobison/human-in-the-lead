## Question

The owner wants the reusable, extensible work-tracking UI in rc.2. Rc.2 was decided to wait only for the Consumer live milestone (HITLD-67) plus HITLD-140.

HITLD-681 is a proposed design item with seven open questions; the chief of staff recommended the smallest slice (the data contract and declarative panels) and warned that all of it delays rc.2.

## Options and recommendation

- A. Smallest slice in rc.2: Walk the design through now; build only the data contract and declarative panels for rc.2; evidence panels and code extensions go to rc.3.
- B. All of it in rc.2: The full design, including evidence panels from adapters, before rc.2; the release waits for it.
- C. Keep it for rc.3: File it under the rc.3 milestone; rc.2's scope stays as decided.

Recommended: Smallest slice in rc.2. The chief of staff recommended it.
