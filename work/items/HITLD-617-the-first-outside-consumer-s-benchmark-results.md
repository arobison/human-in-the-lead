## Question

Are the Cesium benchmark results confidential, or do you just want them kept beside the ledger?

HITLD-609 lists where results live as an open question: kept by the project's tool and referenced by run ID, or also stored in the project's private store.

## Options and recommendation

- A. Confidential: they cannot live in the public repository: Results go in private storage; the public tracker, pull requests and reports carry references only
- B. Preference only: kept beside the ledger: The tool keeps the data and HITLD references run IDs

Recommended: Confidential: they cannot live in the public repository. The chief of staff recommended it.
