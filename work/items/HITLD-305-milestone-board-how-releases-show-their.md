## Question

How should releases show their relationship to the milestones? The owner said the Releases row goes on top and is shown between the milestone swimlanes.

HITLD-62 milestone board UX review (PR #196).

## Options and recommendation

- A. Top row plus a line down: Releases lane on top; each release flag has a vertical line through the lanes at its step.
- B. Edges to needed milestones: Releases lane on top; curved waits from each flag to every milestone it needs.
- C. Flag inside each lane: No separate row; a flag sits in each lane next to the milestones it needs.

Recommended: Top row plus a line down. Shows which milestones land before each release without adding more curves.
