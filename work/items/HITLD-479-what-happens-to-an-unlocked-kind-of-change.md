## Question

When evidence turns up against an unlocked kind of change (a revert, a fix-forward item citing it, a failing check on main, or the owner's report), what happens to it?

Without sampling, the evidence signals are the only check on an unlocked kind.

## Options and recommendation

- A. Re-lock at once, fresh bar: The kind goes back to waiting for the owner's yes immediately and must meet the unlock bar again from zero; the proposal to unlock it comes to the owner as a decision.
- B. Re-lock until the owner re-approves: Back to waiting; no new proposal until the owner says so.
- C. Stay unlocked, flag it: The owner is told at once but the kind keeps merging unless re-locked.

Recommended: Re-lock at once, fresh bar. The chief of staff recommended it.
