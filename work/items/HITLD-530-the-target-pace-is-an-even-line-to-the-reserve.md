## Question

What should the target burn rate be?

HITLD-509: the window ending 2026-11-20 used about 67 percent of the weekly limit in about 75 hours (about 0.9 percent an hour, the owner's other use included), roughly twice an even pace, so the bands fired late and work then idled until the reset.

## Options and recommendation

- A. Even line to the reserve, both ways: A pace line from the window's start to the reserve at the reset; ahead of it the gate slows work early, behind it the system may use spare capacity only on work a commitment allows; the reserve stays untouched; the owner's own use counts as a draw
- B. Even line, slow down only: Being behind the line changes nothing
- C. You set a target each window: The owner declares how much to spend, default the even line

Recommended: Even line to the reserve, both ways. The chief of staff recommended it.
