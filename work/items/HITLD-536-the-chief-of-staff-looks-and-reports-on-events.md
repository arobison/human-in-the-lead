## Question

What should trigger the chief of staff to look, or to tell the owner something?

HITLD-493 and HITLD-89: digests and reports fire on a schedule or a count; the hourly check-in found nothing for over thirty hours running.

## Options and recommendation

- A. Events first, a slow timer as a safety net: Look and report when something changes (a PR's checks finish, an executor goes quiet, a decision is waiting, the pace line is crossed, a usage reading goes stale); a slow timer of every few hours (a setting) only catches a lost event; a digest is sent when something needs the owner, not on the clock
- B. Keep the schedule: Hourly check-ins and a fixed digest
- C. Events only: No timer at all

Recommended: Events first, a slow timer as a safety net. The chief of staff recommended it.
