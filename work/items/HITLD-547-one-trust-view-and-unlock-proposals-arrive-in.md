## Question

What does the owner see of the trust engine?

HITLD-507: several kinds (merging, commitment, goal-choosing, escalation fixes) each have a mode, evidence and a next unlock proposal. HITLD-448 is the dashboard as the owner's view.

## Options and recommendation

- A. One Trust view, and unlock proposals in the owner's queue: A single view lists every kind with its current mode, the evidence behind it and how near it is to its unlock bar; an unlock proposal arrives as a normal decision showing the evidence and a sample; nothing unlocks until the owner answers
- B. Proposals only, no standing view: The owner sees a kind only when proposed for unlocking or re-locked
- C. Per process, in its own place: Each process shows its own trust

Recommended: One Trust view, and unlock proposals in the owner's queue. The chief of staff recommended it.
