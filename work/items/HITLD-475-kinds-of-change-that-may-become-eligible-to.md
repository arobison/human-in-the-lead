## Question

Which kinds of change may become eligible to merge without asking once they have earned it?

Everything not eligible keeps waiting for the owner's yes. The four never-unlock kinds are excluded already (HITLD-474).

## Options and recommendation

- A. All four may become eligible: Docs, comments and wording; tests and fixtures; small code changes with their own tests; dashboard and report presentation.
- B. Fewer: The owner names which.

Recommended: All four may become eligible. The chief of staff recommended it.
