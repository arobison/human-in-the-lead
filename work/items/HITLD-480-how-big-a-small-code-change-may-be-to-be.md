## Question

How big may a small code change be to be eligible to merge without asking? A change over either limit would always wait.

The chief of staff proposed fixed limits on changed lines and files.

## Options and recommendation

- A. At most 150 lines, 5 files: Fixed limits.
- B. At most 50 lines, 3 files: Tighter fixed limits.
- C. At most 400 lines, 10 files: Looser fixed limits.
- D. Decide it later: No default until the first kind unlocks.

Recommended: At most 150 lines, 5 files. The chief of staff recommended it.
