## Question

Accept touches and tier_override being always on in the files tracker's layout (HITLD-286)?

Changes a default of the reference tracker format, the data-model design text and the existing-tracker guide. The old default refused a key the contract tells executors to use. Reverting is one constant.

## Options and recommendation

- A. Yes, always on: Appended to the allowed keys when not listed.
- B. No: keep them off by default: Make the error name optional_keys.

Recommended: Yes, always on. The chief of staff recommended it.
