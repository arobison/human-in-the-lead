## Why

Before a second human or a remote channel answers decisions.

## Done when

- A decision answer carries a signature made with the person's own key.
- An unsigned answer, or one signed by an unknown key, is refused.
- A remote channel's answer counts only when signed.
- Q21 option 4 is recorded in part 22 and the design is updated.
