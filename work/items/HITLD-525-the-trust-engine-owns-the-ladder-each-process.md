## Question

How should merging, commitment and goal-choosing share one trust engine?

HITLD-507: both ladders (HITLD-472 for merging, HITLD-291 for commitment) came out with the same shape, and HITLD-506 decided commitment uses the merging machinery. The design records trust today only as counts per area (part 2.15).

## Options and recommendation

- A. Engine owns the ladder; each process registers kinds: The engine owns the rungs, the unlock bar, the re-lock on a miss, the trust record and the owner's unlock proposals; each process registers its kinds of decision, its evidence signals and its verifiability rule and asks whether it may proceed at a rung; a new process is a registration
- B. One engine, one fixed rule set: All processes use the same kinds and evidence
- C. Keep them separate: Each keeps its own machinery

Recommended: Engine owns the ladder; each process registers kinds. The chief of staff recommended it.
