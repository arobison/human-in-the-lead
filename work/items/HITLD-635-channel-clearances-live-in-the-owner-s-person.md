## Question

Where are the channel clearances recorded (which models, tools and storage places are cleared for what)?

HITLD-620: clearances per channel (HITLD-625, 630); settings already come in layers (person, machine, project) and the strictest layer wins.

## Options and recommendation

- A. In person-level settings, projects may only narrow: One list in the settings file outside every repository naming each channel and its clearance; a project may add a narrower limit in its committed policy, never a wider one; changing a clearance is the owner's recorded decision
- B. In each project's committed policy: Each project lists its own clearances
- C. In the private store: Synced and sealed, readable only where the store is

Recommended: In person-level settings, projects may only narrow. The chief of staff recommended it.
