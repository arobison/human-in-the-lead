## Question

When a kind of change is proposed for unlocking, what should the proposal show so the owner's yes is informed?

HITLD-478: a kind is proposed for unlocking after 15 merges that each aged 7 days with no evidence against, from at least 8 distinct items. HITLD-472 left open what the proposal shows.

## Options and recommendation

- A. The evidence and a sample: The merges with ages and checks, the evidence signals looked for and not found, five of the changes in full, and what the kind would cover from now on
- B. Just the numbers: Counts and ages only
- C. Everything, every time: All 15 changes in full

Recommended: The evidence and a sample. The chief of staff recommended it.
