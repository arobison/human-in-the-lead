## Question

What describes an executor in a more general system than tiers?

The owner finds tiers odd. Design 5.1 decided mode, cascade and budget but not built.

## Options and recommendation

- A. Capability dimensions, mode derived from needs: Lifetime (in-process, detached, own session), interaction (alone, asks, steered), isolation (shared, own worktree), plus model, effort and budget; mode names are common combinations.
- B. Keep 5.1's three parts as decided: Mode, cascade, budget.
- C. Let the item or shaper write the whole spec: Item states its spec.

Recommended: Capability dimensions, mode derived from needs. The chief of staff recommended it.
