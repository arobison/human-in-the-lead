## Question

For back-and-forth work such as dashboard tweaks, should the owner get the executor's session directly instead of relaying through the chief of staff and the orchestrator?

Relaying every tweak is slow (HITLD-315).

## Options and recommendation

- A. Yes, direct session: The orchestrator starts it as an interactive dispatch and the chief of staff gives the owner the session link; it stays a tracked dispatch.
- B. No, keep relaying: Every tweak goes through the chief of staff and the orchestrator.

Recommended: Yes, direct session. Fast iteration without a relay, still tracked.
