## Question

How should today's standing delegations (tracker-only merges, reversible tracker edits) migrate into the one trust engine?

The chief of staff merges tracker-only pull requests on the owner's standing rule; HITLD-143 (reversible tracker edits by the chief of staff and orchestrator without a per-change approval) and HITLD-218 (the merge routine) describe it. HITLD-507: one engine for merging, commitment and the other processes.

## Options and recommendation

- A. Record them as your initial grants in the engine: Tracker-only merges and reversible tracker edits become kinds already unlocked by a decision the owner signs dated now and citing the standing rule, with the same evidence and re-lock as any kind; HITLD-143 and 218 become the registration of those kinds
- B. Reset them to ask-first and re-earn: Every kind starts at ask-first
- C. Keep them as special rules outside the engine: Leave the delegations as separate settings

Recommended: Record them as your initial grants in the engine. The chief of staff recommended it.
