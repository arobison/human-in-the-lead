## Question

Should plan audits be a named job of the curator, with model and effort chosen per job type by the dispatcher's cascade and never by the curator?

HITLD-586: the fuller curator lands in rc.4. The curator's existing pass reads the open items against recent merges and decisions; the audit of 2026-11-19 was a broader version of that and needed the chief of staff's filter before it could be shown (it put HITLD-203 on a start list although an executor cannot write a model-ID trailer, and missed that HITLD-97 carries authors). HITLD-581: model and effort come from a cost-ordered cascade.

## Options and recommendation

- A. Yes, audits included, dispatcher picks the model: Three jobs: upkeep after merges, applying decided changes, plan audits (coverage of milestones and releases, contradictions between items and decisions, duplicates, drift between docs and decisions, proposed start lists); each job type has its own cascade starting rung and first-pass-yield measure; an audit result is claims with evidence that the chief of staff verifies before showing the owner
- B. Audits stay with the chief of staff: The curator does upkeep and applies decided changes only
- C. Audits yes, but the curator picks its own effort: The curator decides how hard to look

Recommended: Yes, audits included, dispatcher picks the model. The chief of staff recommended it.
