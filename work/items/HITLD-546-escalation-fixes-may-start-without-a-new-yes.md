## Question

Who may start fixing an escalation, given that normal starts need the owner's approved list?

HITLD-441: an escalation is also work. A wrong token record held every start until HITLD-436, 438 and 439 were ordered first. Design 4.6: the system never grants itself autonomy; HITLD-474: some kinds never unlock.

## Options and recommendation

- A. Escalation fixes may start without a new yes, within a cap: The fix for a declared escalation may start at once in the reserved slot within a small budget per escalation; anything over that budget, any one-way-door change or anything on a never-unlock kind still waits for the owner's yes; the owner is told when it starts; it is its own kind in the trust engine so standing can grow or shrink on evidence
- B. Always ask first: An escalation jumps the queue in order but nothing starts until approved
- C. Start freely, tell the owner after: Any escalation fix starts at once

Recommended: Escalation fixes may start without a new yes, within a cap. The chief of staff recommended it.
