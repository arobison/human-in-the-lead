## Question

Should the Roadmap's columns follow the release stages, with each release flag at the right edge of its stage?

Today every milestone is in Step 1 and the four release flags stack in one column.

## Options and recommendation

- A. Yes, columns follow releases: A milestone sits in the column of the earliest release that needs it; each flag at the right edge of its column.
- B. Keep step columns: Columns stay milestone-needs only.

Recommended: Yes, columns follow releases. The chief of staff recommended it.
