## Question

Should a kind of commitment move up a rung by the same machinery as merging, with commitment evidence?

HITLD-472 set the merge unlock bar (15 aged merges from 8 distinct items; re-lock at once after a miss). Commitment evidence: finished within budget, estimate close to measured, an item later dropped or abandoned as the wrong work, the owner's report.

## Options and recommendation

- A. Same machinery: One trust engine for merging and commitment; a miss drops that kind a rung.
- B. Own bar for commitments: A separate bar and rules.

Recommended: Same machinery. The chief of staff recommended it.
