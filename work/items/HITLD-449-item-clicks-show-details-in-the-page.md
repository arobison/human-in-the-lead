## Question

Should clicking an item in the dashboard show its details in the page instead of linking to the repository?

Item links go to the forge and could not be followed from the viewer.

## Options and recommendation

- A. Yes, details in the page: Items open in a drawer in the page; the repository link stays as a secondary source link.
- B. Keep the links: Items link out.

Recommended: Yes, details in the page. The chief of staff recommended it.
