## Question

For decision HITLD-617, what does "runs will be gone" mean?

HITLD has no run object (HITLD-484). The measurement-tool design item (HITLD-609) and decision HITLD-617 spoke of run IDs and benchmark runs.

## Options and recommendation

- A. The results themselves go away: Stored results are ephemeral
- B. Drop the word and concept of a run: Use result or measurement; the existing names (bench_runs, getRun, startRun, baseline runs) are renamed in the design
- C. Something else: Ask the owner to say more

Recommended: Drop the word and concept of a run. The chief of staff recommended it.
