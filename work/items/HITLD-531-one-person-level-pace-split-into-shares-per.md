## Question

How should several projects, and the owner's own use outside HITLd, share one account's pace?

The owner asked how the even line works when the same budget (an Anthropic account) is used at the same time by several projects, which may or may not be managed by HITLd, plus interactive use and on-off use. HITLD-490: raw ledgers stay per project and only aggregates cross projects.

## Options and recommendation

- A. One person-level pace, split into shares: One moving target on the account's total, recomputed continuously; use outside HITLd is the measured remainder and has priority; the HITLd portion is split into a share per project by the owner's weights, default an even split among projects with an active commitment; every project's gate reads the same total
- B. A fixed slice per project: Each project gets a set share and ignores the others
- C. Each project follows the line alone: No split; projects can overshoot together

Recommended: One person-level pace, split into shares. The chief of staff recommended it.
