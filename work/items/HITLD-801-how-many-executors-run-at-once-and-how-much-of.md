## Question

The weekly quota is 58.7 percent used and the economize gate held the run at one executor. Do we raise the cap to 5 and spend the reserve down?

The cap is the smallest of the person, machine, project and run layers, and the quota gate lowers it in economize mode. The owner asked for 5 executors, then said the default 25 percent reserve is too much and should be 10 percent.

## Options and recommendation

- A. Cap 5 and spend the reserve down, for a defined window: Person and machine say 5, the reserve is 10 percent and the economize threshold is 20 percent; the project and run caps follow.
- B. Keep the cap and the reserve: Nothing changes; the gate holds the run at one executor.

Recommended: Cap 5 and spend the reserve down, for a defined window. The chief of staff recommended it.
