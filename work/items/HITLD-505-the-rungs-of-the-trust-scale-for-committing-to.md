## Question

What rungs should the scale of trust have for each kind of commitment?

All kinds of commitment are on a scale of trust (HITLD-504); each kind earns its own rung.

## Options and recommendation

- A. Three rungs: Ask first; tell the owner then proceed unless they object within a window; proceed and report afterward. Held items stay held at every rung.
- B. Two rungs: Ask first, or proceed and report.
- C. Four rungs: Adds a silent rung.

Recommended: Three rungs. The chief of staff recommended it.
