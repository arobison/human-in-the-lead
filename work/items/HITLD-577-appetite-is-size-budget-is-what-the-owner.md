## Question

What should the words appetite, budget and pace each mean?

HITLD-412 sets an appetite per milestone from item estimates; HITLD-509 used appetite for the share of the plan HITLd may use; HITLD-291 uses budget for what a goal may spend.

## Options and recommendation

- A. Appetite is size, budget is what you allow, pace is the rate: Appetite: how big a milestone is estimated to be from its item estimates (HITLD-412); budget: what the owner allows a signed commitment to spend (HITLD-522); pace: the rate the plan allows per hour, the even line (HITLD-530)
- B. Appetite is the budget: One word for both
- C. Pick other words: The owner names the terms

Recommended: Appetite is size, budget is what you allow, pace is the rate. The chief of staff recommended it.
