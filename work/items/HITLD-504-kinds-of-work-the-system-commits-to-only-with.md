## Question

Which kinds of work should the system never commit to without the owner's own yes, even inside a goal's budget?

The owner answered that none of this is permanent: all of it is on a scale of trust.

## Options and recommendation

- A. Releases and public promises: Cutting a release, or anything that changes what users are promised.
- B. One-way-door builds: Data formats, store formats, public commands and settings, security and rights.
- C. Items you have held: Any item or area the owner has held stays held.
- D. An item that is a big share of the budget: A single item whose estimate is a large share of the goal's budget.

Recommended: One-way-door builds. The chief of staff recommended it.
