## Question

Once a kind of change is unlocked, how many of its merges should be queued for the owner's after-the-fact review?

A sampled review gives real signal at bounded cost. The chief of staff recommended 1 in 10.

## Options and recommendation

- A. 1 in 10: A random tenth, shown as a short list at a quiet moment.
- B. 1 in 5: More coverage, more owner attention.
- C. 1 in 20: Light touch.
- D. No sampling: Trust rests on the evidence signals only.

Recommended: 1 in 10. The chief of staff recommended 1 in 10; the owner chose no sampling.
