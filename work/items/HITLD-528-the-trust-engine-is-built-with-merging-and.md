## Question

HITLD-527 made the engine's concept supervision modes with an order; merging (HITLD-472) has mechanical evidence and the more settled design, commitment (HITLD-291) has weaker evidence (budget, estimates, dropped work). The chief of staff recommended merging first, as the smaller first step.

## Options and recommendation

- A. Merging first, commitment second: Merging proves the engine, commitment registers next; both written out on paper against the engine before any code
- B. Both at once: Build the engine with merging and commitment together; tests the generality immediately but is the biggest first step
- C. Commitment first: Start where the larger autonomy gain is

Recommended: Merging first, commitment second. The chief of staff recommended it.
