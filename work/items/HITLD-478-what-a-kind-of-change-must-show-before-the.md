## Question

With no sampling, what must a kind of change show before the system proposes unlocking it?

A bare count ignores variety and age. Without sampling, defects only surface by themselves, so merges need time to prove out.

## Options and recommendation

- A. 15 aged merges, 8 distinct items: A merge counts only after 7 days with no evidence against it; the 15 come from at least 8 distinct items; any evidence against resets that kind's count.
- B. 30 aged merges, 12 distinct items: Stricter: 14 days of age each.
- C. 10 aged merges, 5 distinct items: Faster: 3 days of age each.
- D. Keep a bare count of 20: No age or variety requirement.

Recommended: 15 aged merges, 8 distinct items. The chief of staff recommended it.
