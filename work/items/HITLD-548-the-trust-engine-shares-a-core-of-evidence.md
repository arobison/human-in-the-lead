## Question

Which evidence signals does the trust engine share across processes, and which stay with a process?

HITLD-507: merging's evidence is a revert, a fix-forward item, a failing check on main or the owner's report (HITLD-476); commitment's is finished within budget, an estimate close to the measured cost, work later dropped as wrong, and the owner's report (HITLD-506).

## Options and recommendation

- A. Shared core, plus per-process signals: The engine owns the signals every process has (the owner's report that a decision was wrong, a re-opened or reverted outcome, aging without objection); each process registers its own on top; the owner's report is always a miss in any kind
- B. All per process: The engine owns only the bar and the re-lock
- C. Only the shared ones: Every process uses just the owner's report, reverts and aging

Recommended: Shared core, plus per-process signals. The chief of staff recommended it.
