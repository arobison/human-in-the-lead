## Why

When several people lead together, each brings judgment for some loops: a UX expert for the UX loop, a security lead for the security loop. How does a judgment call find the right person? Options to weigh: route by loop and area through decision rights (the lead delegates a loop or area to a person); route each surfaced call by its basis (taste, security, legal) to whoever holds that basis; or the lead triages and delegates call by call, with precedent learning the routing. Also: how each person's feedback loops and calibration (part 23) work per loop, how disagreements between leads are settled, and attention budgets per person. Depends on signed answers (HITLD-36) and on the people-in-the-lead work (part 23). Related: part 4.5, the "Several humans in the lead" milestone (HITLD-53).
