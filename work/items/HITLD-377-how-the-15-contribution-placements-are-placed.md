## Question

Place the 15 items the proposal marked as contributions as constraints of the milestone clause they serve, instead of contributions with placeholder values?

Contributions need a value and a confidence; the value unit (Q18) is open, and several of the 15 are needed by current work, so they should not be cut as lowest value.

## Options and recommendation

- A. As constraints: Each is a constraint of the clause it serves; no invented values; a separate item asks whether contributions stay in the design.
- B. Contributions with placeholder values: Values 1 to 5 at confidence 0.5, revised when shaped (HITLD-374).
- C. Some of each: Each is put to the owner.

Recommended: As constraints. The chief of staff recommended it.
