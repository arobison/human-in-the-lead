## Question

Do milestone contributions (optional items with a value and a confidence) stay in the design?

Nothing uses them; the value unit (Q18) is undefined; the planning pass placed everything as constraints.

## Options and recommendation

- A. Drop them: Everything under a milestone is a constraint; optional work goes to a later milestone; the concept leaves the design, schemas, check, dashboard and planner.
- B. Keep them: Settle the value unit first.

Recommended: Drop them. The chief of staff recommended it.
