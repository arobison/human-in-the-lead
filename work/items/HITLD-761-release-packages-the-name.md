## Question

What should HITLD call the unit that has its own version, tags and release record?

The owner found 'line' unclear. Lerna, Nx, pnpm, Cargo and Go all use package.

## Options and recommendation

- A. Package: The word the monorepo tools use.
- B. Component: Neutral.
- C. Keep line: HITLD-only vocabulary.

Recommended: Package. The chief of staff recommended it.
