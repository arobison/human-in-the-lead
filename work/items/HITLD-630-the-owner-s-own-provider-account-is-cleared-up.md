## Question

What is the default clearance of the model channels?

HITLD-620: the chief of staff on a remote model on the owner's own account already reads confidential material; labels plus channel clearances (HITLD-625); enforcement at the commands that return data (HITLD-629).

## Options and recommendation

- A. Own account up to confidential; others need a grant: The remote model on the owner's own account is cleared for public, internal and confidential, not for secret or labels that say no remote model or nobody; a local model is cleared for anything except nobody; any other provider needs the owner's decision
- B. Remote models up to internal: Confidential needs a grant per label
- C. Every model channel needs an explicit grant: Nothing is cleared by default

Recommended: Own account up to confidential; others need a grant. The chief of staff recommended it.
