## Question

How should the self-learning loop (the chief of staff's introspection on its interactions with the owner and its delegates) start?

The owner asked how to start building self-learning, for example the chief of staff identifying sources of friction and proposing improvements. Design part 18 stage 2 proposes changes to itself; implicit friction signals are a later milestone (HITLD-154, 48 under HITLD-408). The system must not grade its own work (part 18.3), so a separate read-only agent reads the records.

## Options and recommendation

- A. Run the delegated retrospective now: A read-only curator-style job over this run's record: a deterministic extract of the owner's messages, the chief of staff's questions, errors and restarts, then a larger-model agent finds friction as claims with evidence and proposes fixes as proposed items; the chief of staff verifies the claims before showing the owner; later steps follow from what it finds
- B. Build the signal tags first: Tag corrections, repeats and drops as ledger events from now on, retrospective later
- C. Leave it for the curator in rc.4: Fold into the fuller curator as a fourth job

Recommended: Run the delegated retrospective now. The chief of staff recommended it.
