## Question

Who may change a classification label?

HITLD-620: the owner chose labels plus channel clearances (HITLD-625) and inheritance with a strictest default (HITLD-626). Making benchmark results public someday is a loosening.

## Options and recommendation

- A. Anyone may tighten, only the owner may loosen: Any agent or the system may raise a label; lowering one is the owner's signed, recorded decision with a reason
- B. Only the owner, in both directions: Every change waits for the owner
- C. Orchestrator may loosen inside a guardrail: The owner sets a guardrail and the orchestrator loosens within it

Recommended: Anyone may tighten, only the owner may loosen. The chief of staff recommended it.
