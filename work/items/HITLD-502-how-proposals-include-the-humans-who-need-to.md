## Question

How should I handle the note that proposals must include all the humans who need to contribute, not just the owner?

HITLD-430 deferred the several-humans design (HITLD-44) until after v0.1.0. Per-person attribution (HITLD-36, HITLD-409) does not exist yet.

## Options and recommendation

- A. Record it, build a light version: Add the requirement to HITLD-44 and the Proposals view's design now; a proposal carries a list of the people to include and their input is recorded under their own names; deciding stays with the owner until HITLD-44 is decided.
- B. Pull HITLD-44 forward: Do the several-humans walk-through before v0.1.0.
- C. Just record the note: Write it into HITLD-44 as a requirement with no change to scope or timing.

Recommended: Record it, build a light version. The chief of staff recommended it.
