## Question

Set quota.critical_below to 5% instead of 10%, since at 65% used the headroom is exactly 10%?

At 10% the gate falls back to critical path only after one more point of usage.

## Options and recommendation

- A. 5%: Economize mode holds until within 5 points of the reserve; reverted after the planning pass.
- B. Keep 10%: Works only while usage stays at or under 65%.

Recommended: 5%. The chief of staff recommended it.
