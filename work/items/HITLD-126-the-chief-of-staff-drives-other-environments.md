## Why

In the store demo the owner carried about ten prompts between three sessions in two environments, and had to answer each decision in the session that held it. The chief of staff exists to be the one session the owner talks to; the harness can create sessions in a named environment and send them messages.

## Done when

- A design (a part of design 0001 13.1, or its own document) settles: the chief of staff starting and messaging sessions in other environments; whether owner answers are recorded where the owner talks and applied elsewhere from the store (which needs the chief of staff to hold a device key) or stay in the session that holds the decision; and how a relayed answer is still never taken as the owner's.
- The owner decides its questions; build items follow.
