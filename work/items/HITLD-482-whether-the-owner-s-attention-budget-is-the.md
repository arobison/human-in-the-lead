## Question

Should the owner's attention budget be the control knob for what merges without asking?

The owner said size is the wrong measure and to think about agents and what is aware of the attention budget. The chief of staff proposed verifiability as the gate and a review budget to rank what comes to the owner.

## Options and recommendation

- A. Verifiability gates it, your budget ranks it: Only verifiable, revertible changes outside the never-unlock kinds are eligible; a review budget ranks which of those come to the owner.
- B. Verifiability gates it, no budget: Eligible changes merge on green without a ranking; the owner's attention goes only to ineligible ones.
- C. Neither: back to a fixed rule: A class table and a size limit.

Recommended: Verifiability gates it, your budget ranks it. The chief of staff recommended a budget-ranked review; the owner chose verifiability as the gate with no budget.
