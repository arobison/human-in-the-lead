## Question

Should how the owner likes to work be its own record, separate from trust and precedent, with the rule that a preference can never widen what the system may do on its own?

The owner asked whether harness memory is enough for the chief of staff to remember how the owner likes to work, since the system may run from different harnesses; the owner has had to repeat rules such as asking one question at a time across context resets. The design has precedent (standing answers to project decisions, which carry authority) and a person file of settings.

## Options and recommendation

- A. Separate record, preferences never grant authority: A person-level record of working preferences (style and process) written from the owner's own words or confirmed proposals, loaded by the chief of staff in any harness; anything that would widen what the system does alone becomes a trust unlock proposal; harness memory is only a cache
- B. Fold it into precedent: One mechanism, but a style preference would carry authority
- C. Rely on harness memory: Each harness remembers what it can

Recommended: Separate record, preferences never grant authority. The chief of staff recommended it.
