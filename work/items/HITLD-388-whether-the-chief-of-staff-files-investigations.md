## Question

Should the chief of staff file investigations and bug reports into the work tracker on its own when it sees an error repeat, without being asked?

The owner had to ask whether a ticket existed for a repeated check-run status anomaly.

## Options and recommendation

- A. Yes, file them itself: The chief of staff checks for a duplicate, files a proposed investigation or bug item with evidence, and tells the owner after.
- B. Only when asked: The chief of staff files only on request.

Recommended: Yes, file them itself. The chief of staff recommended it.
