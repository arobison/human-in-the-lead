## Question

What should be the unit of autonomy for committing to work: what the owner signs off once instead of each start?

The owner said committing to work is another trust system: eventually the system works against budgets on its own to complete goals by committing to the right work. Related: HITLD-291 (an owner mode for starting runs), HITLD-472 (the trust signal for merging).

## Options and recommendation

- A. A goal under a budget: The owner signs off goals and budgets; the system commits to the items that serve the goal, within the budget, in critical-path order.
- B. An approved list of items: A standing list approved by the owner, started in planner order.
- C. An open mandate within a budget: Any item the planner ranks, within a budget, with no goal.
- D. Tell me more first: The chief of staff explains what each means.

Recommended: A goal under a budget. The chief of staff recommended it.
