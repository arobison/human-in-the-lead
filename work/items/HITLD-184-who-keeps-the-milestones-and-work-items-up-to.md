## Question

Who should update the milestones and work items as they change, until the curator agent of HITLD-132 exists?

HITLD-132 builds a tracker upkeep sweep and a curator pass over work items. Until it exists, items and milestones go stale when work lands, scope changes or a decision is made. Raised for HITLD-155.

Reversibility: two-way.

## Options and recommendation

- A. The chief of staff updates them as they change: The chief of staff keeps states, constraints, notes and links current as work lands and decisions are made, through the normal pull requests, until the curator agent exists.
- B. Only when the owner asks: Items and milestones change only on the owner's request.
- C. Leave it to the executors: Each executor updates its own item and nothing else is kept current.

Recommended: The chief of staff updates them as they change. Nothing else is positioned to see all the changes, and the curator agent is not built yet.
