## Question

What is the right way to bound how much the system asks the owner?

HITLD-493: the design caps interruptions (attention.interrupts_per_day_max 6, decisions_per_digest_max 10); whether the chief of staff's own questions count against them was unchecked.

## Options and recommendation

- A. Count every ask, and let evidence lower it: Every question to the owner counts, including the chief of staff's; the system tracks asks per decision settled and per hour, and the trust engine's unlocks reduce them over time; walk-throughs the owner starts do not count against it; a budget the owner sets stays as a ceiling for system-initiated asks
- B. Keep the fixed daily numbers: 6 interrupts and 10 decisions per digest, counting the chief of staff
- C. No limit, you manage it: No cap

Recommended: Count every ask, and let evidence lower it. The chief of staff recommended it.
