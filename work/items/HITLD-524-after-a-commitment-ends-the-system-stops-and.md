## Question

When a commitment ends (goal met, budget spent, or the gate says stop), what does the system do next?

HITLD-291 asked who proposes the next one; HITLD-470 and 492 dropped the run object, so a commitment ends and there is no run to end. HITLD-505: three rungs; HITLD-506: one trust engine.

## Options and recommendation

- A. Stop, and the chief of staff proposes the next one: The commitment ends; the chief of staff puts the next goal and budget in the owner's queue as a proposed commitment decision; idle only while waiting on the owner or the gate, and says which
- B. Carry on to the next goal automatically: Moves to the next goal in plan order under the same budget rules
- C. Nothing is proposed: It stops and waits until asked

Recommended: Stop, and the chief of staff proposes the next one. The chief of staff recommended it.
