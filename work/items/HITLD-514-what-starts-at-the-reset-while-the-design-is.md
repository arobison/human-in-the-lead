## Question

At the reset, what should the successor orchestrator start while the design is still being settled?

HITLD-512 and HITLD-513 put all open design walk-throughs before any release item is built. The weekly usage window refills at the reset and unused budget is lost; design walk-throughs use almost none of it.

## Options and recommendation

- A. Only items no open design can change: The chief of staff audits the open items against the open designs and gives the owner the list none of them can change; the owner approves it and only those start
- B. Nothing starts: No build work until every walk-through is settled; the window goes unused
- C. Start the rc.2 items as planned: Design first applies from rc.3 on

Recommended: Only items no open design can change. The chief of staff recommended it.
